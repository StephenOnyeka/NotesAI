Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

A minimal, powerful note-taking experience enhanced by AI — built for speed and simplicity.

AI-Powered Assistant

Describe what you want in plain language — typed or spoken — and Gemini generates formatted notes or checklists instantly.

Rich Text Editing

Write with headings, bold, italic, strikethrough, bullet & numbered lists, and code blocks through a powerful TipTap editor.

Smart To-Dos

Interactive checklists with completion toggles, priorities, due dates, and live progress tracking to stay on top of everything.

Blazing Fast Storage

Ultra-fast, synchronous, offline-first persistence with MMKV. Your data stays on your device — no cloud dependency required.

Search, Pin & Organize

Filter notes instantly, pin important ones, and tag them with colors so you always find what you need, fast.

Adaptive Design

Glassmorphic UI with automatic light/dark mode support that adapts beautifully to your system preferences.

How It Works

Three steps to smarter notes.

1

Tell the AI what you need

Type or speak a prompt like "Summarize my meeting notes" or "Create a workout checklist."

2

AI generates your content

Gemini creates a beautifully formatted note or actionable to-do list in seconds.

3

Organize and access offline

Pin, tag, search, and edit your notes — everything is saved locally on your device, always available.
